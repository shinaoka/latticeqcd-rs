## Complexity and ownership audit

Each rule examines only its local bounded inputs: four action tangent slots or
`4+n` JVP inputs where `n<=4`. It performs one bounded allocation and emits at
most one extension node. No rule scans/clones a graph, formats a program, or
constructs an executor, runtime, backend, or AD context. `ad_rules()` creates a
small owned registry once per application request; execution owners remain
explicit. There is no process-global or thread-local state and no inactive-zero
tensor materialization.
